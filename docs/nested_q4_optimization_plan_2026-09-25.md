# G6Q-OPT1 — Nested q4 quantum optimization and five-seed confirmation

## Objective

Determine whether rigorous training-only selection of the retained shallow
four-qubit VQC can beat classical heads receiving the identical q4 coordinates
and improve the clinical-plus-quantum system beyond the frozen all-classical
OOF benchmark (`0.83802` AUPRC).

The experiment tests optimization, loss alignment and input bandwidth. It does
not reopen the failed wider-qubit, residual-routing, advanced-fusion or
structured-clinical branches.

## Evidence motivating the search

- Full-data h128 logistic: `0.83328` AUPRC.
- Full-data PLS-q4 logistic: `0.82698` AUPRC.
- Full-data q4 MLP assistant: `0.83085` AUPRC.
- Best VQC: `0.82747` AUPRC.
- Clinical + curriculum VQC: `0.83697` AUPRC.
- Clinical + q4 MLP: `0.83802` AUPRC.

Thus q4 loses some global/local geometry but retains most MI discrimination.
The remaining system gap is approximately `0.00105` AUPRC. Generic q8/q12/q16
circuits, orthogonal mixing, residual learning, neural fusion and clinical
re-uploading have already failed their gates.

## Nested selection

For each outer fold `k` in folds 1–8:

1. Outer validation is fold `k` and is never inspected during tuning.
2. Inner validation is the next official fold cyclically; the other six folds
   are inner training.
3. Robust scaling, PLS-q4 and quantile-angle mapping are fitted on inner
   training only for search, then refitted on all seven outer-training folds.
4. Inner training uses at most 750 patient-unique records per class. Final
   outer training uses at most 2,000 patient-unique records per class.
5. Inner AUPRC chooses one frozen candidate and epoch count. The selected
   configuration is retrained from scratch before outer prediction.

## Quantum search space

The circuit remains four qubits, two data-upload/variational layers, trainable
`RZ-RY-RZ`, ring `IsingZZ`, local `Z/ZZ` observables and linear readout. The
bounded candidates vary only mechanisms with a direct hypothesis:

- Adam learning rate: `0.002` or `0.005`;
- initial trainable input bandwidth: `0.5`, `1.0` or `1.5`;
- BCE versus clinical-teacher Jensen-Shannon regularization;
- differentiable pairwise ranking weight: `0`, `0.15` or `0.30`;
- epoch selected from 5–30 using inner AUPRC.

Pairwise ranking is used because AUPRC depends on score ordering. BCE remains
present in every arm. The clinical teacher is generated by official-inner-fold
OOF prediction and is used only as a training regularizer.

The search deliberately excludes more depth, dense entanglement and global
measurements. Deep unstructured circuits and global kernels can produce flat
training landscapes or concentration. Data re-uploading remains the selected
explicit model family.

## Matched controls and saved trace

Every outer fold and seed saves:

- selected hyperparameters and epoch;
- q4 coordinates;
- all four local `Z` and four ring `ZZ` observables;
- raw VQC probability;
- no-entanglement VQC probability;
- tuned q4 logistic, 43-parameter MLP and calibrated RBF probabilities;
- clinical-teacher probability and cross-fitted fusion scores;
- patient/sample/transform hashes, gradient ranges and parameter counts.

Classical hyperparameters are selected on the same inner partition. Folds 9
and 10 remain sealed.

## Parallel execution

Two private Kaggle GPU kernels run concurrently:

- seeds A: `42, 31415, 27182`;
- seeds B: `16180, 14142`.

Both use the same pinned source commit and versioned kernel inputs. A later
CPU aggregation combines complete OOF results; one kernel never learns from
the other's outer scores.

## Promotion rules

Promote only when all hold:

1. mean VQC minus strongest identical-q4 classical control is at least
   `+0.005` AUPRC;
2. paired patient-bootstrap 95% interval is above zero;
3. VQC wins in at least four of five seeds;
4. no-entanglement removal causes a measurable decline;
5. sensitivity at 90% specificity and hard-negative FPR do not materially
   worsen;
6. quantum fusion exceeds `0.83802` and beats the matched all-classical fusion
   with a positive interval.

If these gates fail, stop q4 optimization and report that extensive nested,
multi-seed evidence did not establish a quantum predictive advantage.
